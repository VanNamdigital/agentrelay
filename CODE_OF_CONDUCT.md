# Code of Conduct

AgentRelay contributors are expected to keep discussion respectful, technical, and focused on improving the project.

## Expected Behavior

- Be clear and constructive in issues, pull requests, and reviews.
- Assume good intent while still asking for evidence and tests.
- Respect local-first security goals and user privacy.
- Avoid harassment, insults, spam, or disclosure of private information.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments and contributions that are abusive, unsafe, or unrelated to the project. Repeated or severe violations may result in being blocked from project spaces.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns to the repository owner. Include links, screenshots, or other context when available.
